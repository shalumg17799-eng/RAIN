RAIN platform PRD v0.2 factory extract
This is the build-factory input distilled from the product requirements document version 0.2: the two mvp use cases, the entities of the planned data layer, and the governing policies. The full document remains the human reference.

The platform gives rural health centers, their partners and state programs one platform for remote access to care and population-level insight, starting with smart scheduling with pattern-based reminders and rural population health insights.

The platform manages patient and provider records with clinic_site context for the participating health centers.
Smart scheduling uses schedule, slot and appointment records. Rural patients book, confirm or switch to telehealth with fewer wasted trips.
Reminders use reminder_message and contact_preference data in the patient's own language over text or voice.
Coverage checks use eligibility_check responses before a visit.
Clinical history uses encounter, condition and medication records.
Remote monitoring uses rpm_reading data for blood pressure, glucose, weight and oxygen trends.
Social needs use sdoh_screening responses covering the five core social domains.
Utilization analysis uses claim history for emergency and admission patterns.
Risk and follow-up use risk_score and outreach_task records. Care coordinators get a prioritized follow-up list.
County context uses county_metric, broadband_coverage and travel_time data for access and equity views.

Use case 1, smart scheduling: which patients are likely to miss an appointment, which open slots fit them, and which visits can switch to telehealth?
Use case 2, population health insights: which patients are rising risk across clinical, monitoring, social and utilization signals, and where are the county-level care gaps?

Policy: the mvp runs on synthetic and public data only, labelled as such on every screen.
Policy: patient matching uses name, birth date, address and phone with a manual review queue for uncertain matches.
Policy: the platform flags and routes; clinicians decide.

The platform also needs a no_show_rate entity that is not yet modelled.
